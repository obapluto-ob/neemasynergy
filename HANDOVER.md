## HOW TO USE YOUR ADMIN PANEL

Go to: https://neemasynergy.netlify.app/admin/
Enter your password and log in.

### UPLOADING IMAGES
The admin panel has the following image slots:

| Section | What to upload |
|---|---|
| Logo | Your company logo (PNG recommended) |
| Hero Section | A high quality event photo (1920x1080px) |
| About Image | A photo of you or your team (portrait) |
| Portfolio Images | Photos of past events (9 slots) |
| Services Images | Photos representing each service (6 slots) |
| Team Photos | Headshots of team members (3 slots) |

**To upload:** Click or drag an image onto the upload area.
**To replace:** Upload a new image — it replaces the old one automatically.
**To delete:** Click the Delete button under the image.

### ADDING GOOGLE MAPS
1. Go to Google Maps (maps.google.com)
2. Search your business location
3. Click **Share** → **Embed a map**
4. Copy the URL inside src="..." only
5. Go to Admin → Google Maps
6. Paste the URL and click **Save Map**

### SETTING UP CONTACT FORM EMAIL
So enquiries from the contact form reach your inbox:

1. Go to https://www.emailjs.com and create a FREE account
2. Click **Add New Service** → connect your Gmail or email
3. Click **Email Templates** → **Create New Template**
4. Use these variables in your template:
   - {{from_name}} — sender's name
   - {{from_email}} — sender's email
   - {{phone}} — sender's phone
   - {{service}} — service they're interested in
   - {{event_date}} — their event date
   - {{guest_count}} — number of guests
   - {{message}} — their message
5. Copy your **Public Key** (Account → General)
6. Copy your **Service ID** and **Template ID**
7. Go to Admin → Email Setup
8. Paste all three keys and click **Save & Activate**

From that point all contact form submissions go directly to your inbox.
